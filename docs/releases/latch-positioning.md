# Latch Positioning

**Category:** Private Rooms

**Evidence mapping:** See [`brand-promise-gate-map.md`](brand-promise-gate-map.md) for the PRIVATE / HUMAN / TRUSTWORTHY promise-to-evidence map.

Latch is positioned around privacy for meeting content: E2EE video keeps meeting media encrypted so services do not receive plaintext meeting content or encryption keys. The proof claim is concrete and evidence-oriented: **E2EE video**, supported by ciphertext-on-wire inspection, opaque-SFU review, and the applicable independent security and privacy evidence.

Secondary promises:

- No AI features.
- No meeting-content telemetry.
- No cloud recording or server-side archiving.

This is not a claim that nothing whatsoever leaves the room. Operational metadata, signaling, and ciphertext still transit services as required to establish, route, secure, and operate a meeting. Positioning and release evidence must describe that boundary precisely and must not use an absolute “nothing leaves the room” claim.

Marketing claims require evidence. In particular, “Verified before release” is conditional until the relevant release candidate has all five independent Architecture, Security, Privacy, QA, and Adversarial approvals and the Phase 10 evidence reconciliation and decision are recorded.
