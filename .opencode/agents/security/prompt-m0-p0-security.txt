### 1. SFrame E2EE Threat Model (Criterion 4)
- **Artifact:** `qa/reports/wireshark-livekit-sframe.pcapng` + `qa/reports/tshark-sframe-output.txt`
- **Task:** Verify Wireshark capture shows ciphertext only, no plaintext NALs
- **Checks:**

- **Output:** Security APPROVED sign-off or documented NO-GO
